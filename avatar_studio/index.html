🎬 AI Studio · Talking Avatar

Turn your narration audio into a talking 3D presenter. Processing happens in your browser. Choose a built-in male or female avatar, preview the speech, and record a WebM clip for your video workflow.

Choose a presenter and audio file, then load the presenter.
How recording works

Recording captures the avatar canvas at 30 fps and the browser-tab audio. When prompted, select this tab and enable tab audio. The result is a WebM file. Convert it to MP4 with FFmpeg if your editor requires MP4. Keep the browser tab visible while recording.

This is a 3D avatar, not a photo-realistic portrait. Only facial mouth shapes, blinks and brows animate; there is no full-body movement. Hindi/Hinglish lip-sync can be less precise than English.

The SDK and built-in avatars are MIT-licensed. The avatar/model are fetched from jsDelivr on first use. No avatar account or API key is required. See SDK source and license.
